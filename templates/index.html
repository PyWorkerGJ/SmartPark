Smart Parking Management System

Real-time parking discovery, reservation, and occupancy management — evolved from our original Smart Resource Booking & Management System.

🚗 Launch SmartPark 🛠️ Admin Dashboard

Problem Statement

Parking availability is difficult to determine in real time, leading to unnecessary searching, congestion, inefficient space utilization, and poor overall parking management on campus and in shared facilities. Manual or unmanaged parking leads to double bookings of space, wasted time, and no visibility into actual occupancy.

What is SmartPark?

A centralized platform for parking discovery, slot reservation, live occupancy tracking, sessions, payments and digital receipts — with a real SQLite-backed database driving every number.

Innovation

Every reservation, session, and transaction is a real database record — built to be extendable into a genuine IoT-based smart parking deployment.

System Methodology

Dataset / Input

Parking zones, slots, vehicles, reservations, sessions and transaction data, stored and queried from a real SQLite database.

Model / Architecture

Flask REST API over SQLite, atomic slot-state transitions to prevent double booking, and a sensor-simulation layer that mirrors a future IoT integration.
